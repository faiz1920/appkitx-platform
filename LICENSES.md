# Third-Party Assets & Licenses

Registry of third-party assets adopted by the AppKitX design system and platform.
Every asset used in the product must appear here with its source and license before merge.

| Asset                     | Package                               | License                   | Source                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Inter (variable)          | `@fontsource-variable/inter`          | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | https://github.com/rsms/inter              |
| JetBrains Mono (variable) | `@fontsource-variable/jetbrains-mono` | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |

Both fonts are self-hosted via Fontsource. No proprietary font files are bundled.
No CDN loading.

None. The product uses no stock photography or third-party illustrations.

## Policy

- Any new third-party asset (icon set, illustration, font, image, snippet) requires its
  source and license documented in this file before merge.
- Public accessibility of an asset does not imply a license to use it.
- Uncertain licensing must be flagged for human review rather than assumed cleared.
